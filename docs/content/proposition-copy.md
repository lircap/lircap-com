## Page copy blocks

### `/for-investors/` — For Investors

**Page role**
Help capital holders understand how Lir thinks about opportunity fit without presenting a public menu of investments or implying open access to products.

**Hero eyebrow**
For investors

**Hero headline**
Capital is most effective when the opportunity has already been understood.

**Hero copy**
Lir works with relevant capital networks to assess where a mandate may meet a suitable opportunity. The work is selective, partner-led and built around judgement: understanding the mandate, testing the fit, and progressing only where the opportunity deserves a serious conversation.

**Primary CTA**
Discuss mandate fit

**Secondary CTA**
View sectors and capabilities

**Section: What investors need**
Private capital rarely lacks choice. The harder question is which opportunities are worth attention. Lir helps filter that question before it becomes noise: sector relevance, structure, timing, counterparty fit and the practical path to a decision.

**Section: How Lir helps**

- **Mandate understanding** — We begin with what the investor is actually seeking: sector, structure, scale, timing, risk appetite and decision process.
- **Opportunity assessment** — We test whether an opportunity is mature enough, clear enough and relevant enough to merit further discussion.
- **Counterparty fit** — Where there is alignment, Lir supports a discreet introduction and helps the conversation stay focused on the right issues.
- **Partner-led judgement** — Senior attention is applied early, so weak fit is identified before time is wasted on either side.

**Proof-safe note**
Selected prior work and sector context can be discussed privately where appropriate and permitted. Public case-study or counterparty references should not be added here unless `CL-004` and `CL-005` are cleared.

**Compliance-sensitive alternatives**

| Risky deck-style claim | Web-safe draft | More conservative fallback |
|---|---|---|
| “We find the opportunity.” | “Lir helps assess where a mandate may meet a suitable opportunity.” | “Lir helps determine whether there is a suitable basis for discussion.” |
| “Direct relationships across sovereign funds, family offices, institutional allocators.” | “sovereign, family-office and institutional capital networks” | “relevant capital networks” |
| “Pre-qualified, pre-structured, ready for decision.” | “tested for sector relevance, structure, timing and fit” | “reviewed before being progressed.” |
| “Co-invest alongside you.” | Omit from public proposition copy pending counsel. | Discuss privately only after regulated-activity review. |

**Claim IDs:** `CL-002`, `CL-003`, `CL-008`, `CL-010`, `CL-012`, `CL-013`.

### `/for-deals/` — For Deals

**Page role**
Speak to companies, owners and introducers who need the right capital partner or senior advisory judgement, without promising market access or a close.

**Hero eyebrow**
For deals

**Hero headline**
A good opportunity still needs the right route to capital.

**Hero copy**
Lir works with owners, companies and introducers on opportunities that need careful positioning, credible structure and the right counterparty conversation. Before anything is taken forward, the team looks at the fundamentals: why this opportunity, why now, what capital is needed, and which route is most likely to create alignment.

**Primary CTA**
Discuss an opportunity

**Secondary CTA**
Explore investor fit

**Section: Before the market hears the story**
The strongest opportunities can lose momentum if they are introduced too broadly, too early or without a clear investment narrative. Lir’s role is to slow the first step down: understand the asset or company, test the story, identify the gaps, and decide whether a focused capital conversation is appropriate.

**Section: How Lir engages**

- **Opportunity review** — We assess the business or asset, the capital requirement, timing, constraints and decision-makers.
- **Narrative and structure** — We help sharpen the investment case so the proposition is clear before it reaches counterparties.
- **Capital strategy** — We consider which type of capital relationship may fit, rather than defaulting to broad distribution.
- **Discreet progression** — Where there is a fit, engagement terms and confidentiality expectations are agreed before work progresses.

**Proof-safe note**
Do not include named counterparties, transaction values, active-deal counts or “to be inserted” track-record cards on this page. An anonymised proof unit may be added only after separate review.

**Compliance-sensitive alternatives**

| Risky deck-style claim | Web-safe draft | More conservative fallback |
|---|---|---|
| “We take it to close.” | “Lir helps shape the route to a credible capital conversation.” | “Lir helps assess whether the opportunity is suitable for further discussion.” |
| “Connect your deal to the right capital.” | “identify which type of capital relationship may fit” | “consider potential routes for discussion.” |
| “No scatter-gun distribution.” | “rather than defaulting to broad distribution” | “with a focused approach.” |
| “Formal mandate letters, confidentiality, KYC/AML across every jurisdiction.” | “engagement terms and confidentiality expectations are agreed before work progresses” | “terms are agreed before work progresses.” |

**Claim IDs:** `CL-002`, `CL-004`, `CL-005`, `CL-010`, `CL-014`.

### `/sectors/` — Sectors / Capabilities

**Page role**
Show where Lir has pattern recognition and where conversations may fit, without claiming current active deals, exclusive access, superlative market position or cleared track record.

**Hero eyebrow**
Sectors and capabilities

**Hero headline**
Where judgement has context.

**Hero copy**
Lir is most useful where an opportunity is complex enough to need more than a standard process: a sharper narrative, a more selective capital conversation, or senior judgement about whether the route is viable at all.

**Primary CTA**
Explore fit

**Section: Capability areas**

- **Technology and digital infrastructure** — Companies and assets at inflection points: growth, repositioning, resilience, infrastructure demand or strategic transition.
- **Mining and hard assets** — Situations where asset quality, jurisdiction, timing and capital structure need to be understood together.
- **Construction and real infrastructure** — Mid-market opportunities that may be too specialised for generic capital processes and too substantial for informal introductions.
- **Ports, toll roads and networks** — Infrastructure-style assets where counterparties need clarity on durability, governance and capital fit.
- **Shipping and special situations** — Transitional or stressed situations where timing, asset knowledge and exit logic matter.
- **Capital strategy and advisory** — Selective support for opportunities that need narrative, structure and a focused counterparty conversation.
- **Private markets** — Treated separately and by introduction only; not marketed as a public opportunity set.

**Section: What belongs here**
This page should not read as a catalogue of every sector Lir can name. Each area should earn its place because Lir can bring relevant context, partner attention or a credible route to discussion. If a sector cannot be supported with approved proof or partner confirmation, keep the description broad or remove it.

**Proof-safe note**
Do not publish “active sectors,” “active deals,” sector transaction placeholders or market superlatives. If proof is later added, use anonymised, substantiated proof units linked to `CL-004`, `CL-005`, `CL-006` and `CL-011`.

### `/private-markets/` — Private Markets

**Page role**
If this page is included at all, it should act as a restrained gatekeeping page for existing or directly introduced counterparties. It must not market opportunities, imply exclusivity, describe SPV products, or invite investment.

**Hero eyebrow**
Private markets

**Hero headline**
Private discussions, by introduction only.

**Hero copy**
Lir’s private-markets work is not a public opportunity set. Where appropriate, it is discussed privately with existing counterparties or direct partner introductions, and only after the nature of the counterparty, opportunity and context has been understood.

**Primary CTA**
Discuss privately

**Section: Deliberately limited**
This capability should remain minimal on the public site. The page may explain that certain private-market situations are handled discreetly, but it should not describe available assets, structures, terms, pipeline cadence, eligibility mechanics or investment routes.

**Section: How conversations progress**

- **Introduction first** — Access is not marketed publicly. Conversations begin through an existing relationship or direct partner introduction.
- **Context before content** — Lir assesses the counterparty, the nature of the opportunity and whether a discussion is appropriate before sharing further detail.
- **No public offer** — Website copy must not present any opportunity, product, security, fund, vehicle or invitation to invest.

**Conservative option: omit page**
If counsel does not clear a standalone page, remove `/private-markets/` from navigation and keep only a single reference in Sectors: “Private markets are handled privately and by introduction only.”

**Compliance-sensitive alternatives**

| “Available exclusively to existing counterparties.” | “discussed privately with existing counterparties or direct partner introductions” | “discussed privately where appropriate.” |
| “SPV-matched capital.” | Omit. | Omit. |
| “Weekly opportunity review / active pipeline.” | Omit. | Omit. |

## Cross-page voice and build handoff
